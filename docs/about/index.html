About FitmentChart

FitmentChart is a reference for the specs you need when buying parts or towing: bolt pattern and center bore, towing capacity, engine oil type and capacity, tire sizes, battery group and wiper blade sizes, plus engines, fuel economy, dimensions, recalls and crash ratings, organised by year, make and model.

Every value has a source

Towing, oil, tire, battery and wiper values link to the document they came from, usually an owner's manual page, an OEM towing guide or a manufacturer spec sheet. Values from third-party references are labelled third-party, and battery group sizes that come only from third-party lookups are labelled low confidence. When no source gives a value we leave it blank.

Check before you buy

Manufacturers change parts mid-year and by trim or package. Always confirm against your vehicle's door placard, owner's manual, the label on the part you're replacing, or the part maker's fitment guide.
