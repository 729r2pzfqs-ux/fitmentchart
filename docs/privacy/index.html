Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-14.

What we collect

FitmentChart has no accounts, forms or comments, and does not ask for personal information. The vehicle search runs in your browser; your searches are not sent to us.

Third-party services

  • Google AdSense shows advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ad Settings. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Google Analytics 4 measures page views and general usage (pages visited, approximate location, device type). It uses cookies set by Google.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log IP addresses for security and operations; see GitHub's privacy statement.

FitmentChart does not set its own cookies. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent mode is set to deny advertising and analytics storage by default.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings and use the Google opt-out above. Blocking cookies does not stop the site from working.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
